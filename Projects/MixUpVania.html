Mix Up Vania

Explore different game genres as a Metroidvania protagonist and try to find a way home.

Mix Up Vania was created in a week for the Brackeys Game Jam 2026.1, following the theme "Strange Places". For this theme, we created a platforming game where the player of a Metroidvania breaks out of their game into another and now tries to get back home

I was the programmer of the duo and developed all the systems and mechanics within the game.
